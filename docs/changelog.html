Changelog

What changed in each release, newest first. Downloads are on the releases page.

1.7.0 November 14, 2026 · release

  • Sync an autopost rule. Sync… in a rule's ⋯ menu re-queues its failed autoposts: their clips go back in the pool and upload in the next empty slots. Clips you unscheduled or swapped out stay skipped.
  • Duplicate no longer reposts clips. A copied rule starts with the original's skipped clips and the clips it already has queued, so duplicating a rule and deleting the original doesn't bring them back.

1.6.0 November 14, 2026 · release

  • Analytics page: views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers, a views-per-day chart and your top 10 videos for 7, 28 or 90 days, per channel or all channels together. Turn on YouTube Analytics API once in your Google Cloud project.
  • Fixed: autoposts stuck on "Uploading" at 0% when their Short had already been rendered.
  • Fixed: a backup can no longer put a bad Twitch name into your clip folder path.

1.5.0 November 13, 2026 · release

  • Queue: select several posts to Approve, Upload now, Retry, Duplicate, Unschedule or Delete drafts at once.
  • Select all appears in the selection bar once something is selected.

1.4.0 November 13, 2026 · release

  • New look: the pink ChannelDeck logo in the app, its icon and the menu bar.
  • Duplicate… for autopost rules.

1.3.1 November 13, 2026 · release

  • Fixed: "Delete local files after posting" only deletes inside your clip folder, and keeps any file an unfinished post still needs.

1.3.0 November 13, 2026 · release

  • Calendar: swipe sideways to change week or month.
  • The queue fits the window again, and every time uses the same 12-hour clock.
  • Hide post titles for streaming.
  • Fixed: a video now has to stay missing from YouTube for an hour before it counts as deleted; importing a backup no longer changes Dry Run; the video player can't be pointed outside your folders.

1.2.0 November 13, 2026 · release

  • From clip library… in the post editor, and Post… on every clip.
  • Select all on the Clips page.
  • Fixed: removing a Twitch channel after some of its clips were posted.

1.1.0 November 13, 2026 · release

  • The post editor's Format switch: horizontal, Twitch's vertical version, or a vertical render, with Render now.
  • Autopost rules make Shorts from the landscape clip with their layout.
  • This website's home page and privacy policy.

1.0.0 November 13, 2026 · release

The first numbered version, with everything built in ten phases:

  • Google sign-in for any number of YouTube channels, with encrypted sign-ins.
  • The post editor and the queue.
  • Resumable uploads with Dry Run, retries and quota handling.
  • Status sync, publish-time changes and "needs decision" for missed times.
  • Twitch sign-in and the clip bank with daily sync and backfill.
  • Autopost rules with vertical layouts, Review and Auto modes.
  • Calendar, templates and numbered series.
  • Menu bar icon, quota meter, disk cleanup and backups.
  • The .dmg, the setup checklist and a build self-test.

The full technical changelog is at the bottom of the README.
