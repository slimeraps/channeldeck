Your data

Everything ChannelDeck keeps is on your Mac. Here's where it lives, how to back it up, and how YouTube's quota works.

Where it lives

What Where
Database ~/Library/Application Support/ChannelDeck/channeldeck.db
Google client file ~/Library/Application Support/ChannelDeck/google-client.json
Sign-in tokens Inside the database, encrypted with the macOS Keychain
Twitch clips ~/Movies/ChannelDeck/Clips (you can change it in Settings)
Logs ~/Library/Logs/ChannelDeck/main.log

Logs record what the app did, such as which YouTube or Twitch call ran and whether it worked. Tokens, secrets and sign-in codes are scrubbed before anything is written.

Backups

Settings → Backup → Export backup… saves a JSON file with your settings, YouTube and Twitch channels, templates, series, layouts, autopost rules and posts not yet on YouTube. It never contains sign-in tokens or secrets.

To restore it, for example on a new Mac:

  1. Choose Settings → Backup → Import backup….
  2. Click Reconnect on each channel, since backups carry no sign-ins.
  3. Turn your autopost rules back on. They arrive switched off so nothing posts before you've checked them.

Import adds what's missing and leaves Dry Run and "delete local files after posting" as they are on that Mac. Clips come back from Twitch on the next sync.

YouTube quota

Quota belongs to your Google Cloud project and is shared by all your channels. Both limits reset at midnight Pacific time, and it's free: going over just pauses uploads until the reset.

Limit Per day What uses it
Uploads 100 Each video upload
Quota units 10,000 Thumbnail, playlist add, video update or delete: 50 each. Reading lists and statuses: 1 each.

The sidebar's meter shows today's use. The Autopost page warns you when your rules plus your queue would go over a limit on any day of the coming week.

The Analytics page uses the separate YouTube Analytics API, which has its own quota and doesn't count against these numbers. Its reports are kept for an hour unless you click Refresh.

Disk space

Settings shows how much space each Twitch channel's clips use. Turn on Delete local files after posting to remove clip videos a number of days after every post that uses them is published. ChannelDeck only deletes inside your clip folder and never a file a post still needs.

Deleting everything

  1. Remove your channels in ChannelDeck. That revokes its access to your Google and Twitch accounts.
  2. Quit ChannelDeck.
  3. Delete ~/Library/Application Support/ChannelDeck, your clip folder and ~/Library/Logs/ChannelDeck.

You can also remove access any time from your Google Account permissions or Twitch connections. See the privacy policy for the full details.
